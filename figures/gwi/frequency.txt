Media consumption frequency

Independent visualization of published GWI estimates, not the publisher's original chart.
Source: GWI Q2 2026, DataReportal Digital 2027, pages 38–40.
https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-global-overview-report
Project source table: ../../data/media_use_2026.md

Legend: blue = short videos; grey = other categories. Color does not indicate quality or impact.
Population: internet users aged 16+ in covered markets; Russia excluded. Self-reports, not a census or screen logs. Uncertainty is not shown here.
Short/long videos do not isolate vertical/horizontal orientation. Music is streaming only; TV streaming includes films, series and other programmes. Digital books, standalone films and pictures have no comparable observations; they are omitted, not treated as zeros. Audiences overlap; bars must not be summed.

Games are excluded only from this frequency chart: the source reports device-specific frequency (mobile: 2.80 days; PC/console: 1.73), not all-game frequency. These values must not be added. Days are not sessions.
