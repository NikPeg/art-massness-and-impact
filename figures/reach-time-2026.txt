Reach and consumption time

Source: GWI Q2 2026, reproduced in DataReportal Digital 2027, pages 38 and 40. Internet users aged 16+ in covered markets; Russia excluded. Not a census of all humans.

Axes: reported weekly reach (%) and reported weekly minutes, as transcribed in ../data/media_use_2026.md. No division by reach, multiplication by audience, or time conversion. Data points are not jittered; leader lines connect displaced labels to exact points.

Observations:
Short online video: 88.5% weekly reach; 426 minutes/week. Short online video, not only Instagram Reels
Long online video: 81.2% weekly reach; 299 minutes/week. Long online video proxy; source-defined category; duration cutoff and orientation not isolated
Music streaming: 77.4% weekly reach; 301 minutes/week. Music streaming only
Video games: 74.4% weekly reach; 433 minutes/week. Reach/time all devices; frequency mobile, then PC/console
TV streaming (all content): 72% weekly reach; 308 minutes/week. All TV streaming, a proxy including films and other programs

Omitted because one or both coordinates are unavailable:
Feature films: Feature films not isolated
Digital books: Not reported as a comparable category
Standalone pictures: Pictures not isolated from mixed social-media and platform use

Missing observations are not zeros. Music is streaming-only. TV streaming includes films and other programmes and does not isolate series. Source short/long video categories do not isolate vertical/horizontal orientation. Audiences overlap; embedded components are not added.

The selected point estimates put short video first in reach and games first in time. The 433 versus 426 minute contrast has not been tested for statistical significance. No complete eight-format ranking, causal effect, or life-impact estimate follows.
