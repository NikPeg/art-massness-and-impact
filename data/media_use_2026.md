Other source categories: all TV, including streaming, has 86.1% weekly reach and 598 weekly minutes. These aggregates are not additional disjoint art forms. The game-frequency values overlap and must not be added. Long online video has been promoted from a contextual observation to an explicit proxy row; it is not a disjoint audience. The linked report also gives 3.36 for its weekly frequency. The report prose says "times"; the source-chart convention retained by this dossier is days per week, not sessions. See the [category definition](../research/01_scope_and_metrics.md).

The original chart calls frequency the **average number of days per week**. It must not be described as sessions or number of uses. Older report summaries sometimes use looser language; the chart definition is used here.

The music row measures streaming only, not all online musical contact. Audible music within short vertical video, long horizontal video, online films, series and games is substantive musical exposure. Its union with streaming audiences, audible durations and days is not measured here. The [music scenario table](music_online_scenarios.md) keeps this distinction explicit; the empirical row is not silently increased.
