# Subjective score rationales

Read with the [score table](subjective_scores_2026.md), [source register](../sources/source_register.md), and [limitations](../research/04_limitations.md). These are interpretive justifications, not measured effects. Numeric anchors can concern narrower populations or markets than the artistic category.

| category | axis | rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| short_video | reach | Broad weekly reach in the online-adult survey; not a share of all humanity. |
| short_video | time | A substantial share of daily attention in GWI. |
| short_video | frequency | Highly recurrent short contacts; frequency is days per week. |
| short_video | money | Reels has a large annualized pace; the landmark covers only Meta and is not realized annual revenue. |
| short_video | recognition | Hypothesis: individual clips often have shorter lives than songs or films, although templates and memes can persist. |
| short_video | significance | A brief encounter offers limited room for developed reflection, but a personally relevant clip can be powerful. |
| short_video | beliefs | Repeated exposure can accumulate norms and viewpoints; the rating includes the feed environment. |
| short_video | actions | A clip may trigger action; the incidence of major decisions is unknown. |
| short_video | persistence | An isolated clip may fade; repeated exposure may sustain its effect. |
| short_video | biography | Hypothesis: a single clip is less often recognized as a life chapter; communities and prolonged engagement may be exceptions. |
| music | reach | Streaming is only one channel; the higher whole-music score extrapolates beyond its measured reach. |
| music | time | IFPI's broad listening estimate informs a high score but predates 2026 and overlaps video. |
| music | frequency | Radio and other channels motivate an extrapolation beyond streaming-only frequency. |
| music | money | Recorded-music income is a partial market boundary; concerts are excluded from the anchor. |
| music | recognition | Repeated listening and intergenerational transmission can sustain particular songs. |
| music | significance | Emotional meaning and belonging can be substantial; IFPI reports music's importance to listeners. |
| music | beliefs | Lyrics, performers, and scene values may shape views; magnitude is a judgment. |
| music | actions | Strong emotion need not become a career, relationship, or relocation decision. |
| music | persistence | Repeated listening can return people to the same work over years. |
| music | biography | Music can become associated with periods of life and identity. |
| film | reach | Cinema, TV, and home viewing jointly suggest wide access; feature-only global reach is not established. |
| film | time | Long sessions may coexist with lower everyday regularity than feeds or music. |
| film | frequency | Feature-film consumption is assumed to be comparatively episodic. |
| film | money | The box-office anchor omits streaming, home viewing, and licensing. |
| film | recognition | Established stories, characters, scenes, and quotations can remain recognizable. |
| film | significance | A film can support intense empathy and reflection; the score is not a measured average. |
| film | beliefs | A story can offer another perspective; narrative research does not validate this numerical comparison. |
| film | actions | Identification or reconsideration is possible; consequential action rates are unknown. |
| film | persistence | Memorable scenes and subsequent discussion may sustain effects. |
| film | biography | A particular film can be remembered as an important experience. |
| series | reach | TV-streaming reach is a proxy that also includes films and other programs. |
| series | time | Streaming hours inform the rating; non-streaming series also exist. |
| series | frequency | TV-streaming days are a proxy, not a series-only frequency estimate. |
| series | money | Total Netflix revenue is a narrow company proxy and includes films. |
| series | recognition | Long-lived characters and franchises can sustain recognition. |
| series | significance | Continued contact can build a meaningful relationship with characters. |
| series | beliefs | Recurrent depictions may shift ideas about normal relationships and lifestyles. |
| series | actions | Decision effects are possible, but major life-change probabilities are not established. |
| series | persistence | Extended viewing can accumulate influence, depending on the work. |
| series | biography | Characters and shared viewing can accompany a life period. |
| books | reach | Global fiction-reading reach is not available; the moderate rating is a judgment. |
| books | time | US leisure reading is only a geographically narrow and broader-than-fiction anchor. |
| books | frequency | A reading habit among some people coexists with many irregular readers. |
| books | money | US fiction publisher revenue is not the world book market. |
| books | recognition | Reissues, translations, and adaptations can extend a text's life. |
| books | significance | Engaged reading can provide space for interpreting personal experience. |
| books | beliefs | A developed argument or perspective can be revisited; superiority over other media is not experimentally established here. |
| books | actions | A perspective or practical plan may support a significant decision; the score is not its probability. |
| books | persistence | Rereading and notes provide mechanisms for maintaining influence. |
| books | biography | A book may be explicitly associated with a turning point; this remains an author hypothesis. |
| theater | reach | Broadway attendances illustrate one local market and are neither unique people nor global reach. |
| theater | time | Limited attendance and relatively infrequent visits suggest a small population time budget. |
| theater | frequency | Cost, geography, and event-based attendance suggest episodic use. |
| theater | money | Broadway play gross is a local anchor, not worldwide theater revenue. |
| theater | recognition | A play can persist across repeated and new productions; the unit is the work. |
| theater | significance | Live presence and collective experience may be emotionally intense. |
| theater | beliefs | Staged conflict can expose competing values; the strength is not measured. |
| theater | actions | Discussion and reconsideration may follow; major decision incidence is unknown. |
| theater | persistence | Memory of a meaningful performance can persist. |
| theater | biography | Performance, venue, companions, and context may jointly make an important event. |
| games | reach | GWI directly provides an all-device gaming reach anchor among online adults. |
| games | time | GWI combines mobile, PC, and console gaming time. |
| games | frequency | Mobile and PC/console frequency overlap and must not be summed. |
| games | money | The Newzoo market estimate supports a high commercial-scale judgment. |
| games | recognition | Characters, worlds, and franchises can become durable cultural references. |
| games | significance | Choice, participation, and shared experience may be personally meaningful. |
| games | beliefs | Simulated consequences may offer a perspective; real-world transfer is not guaranteed. |
| games | actions | Practice and collaboration can lead to real activities; major life changes remain unmeasured. |
| games | persistence | Repeated practice and a continuing social environment may sustain influence. |
| games | biography | Friendship, shared play, and communities can become part of personal history. |
| long_video | reach | GWI long online video reaches 81.2% weekly; this broader proxy does not isolate horizontal creative works. |
| long_video | time | The proxy reports 299 weekly minutes, or about 42.7 daily minutes; the score remains an author judgment. |
| long_video | frequency | The long-online-video proxy informs recurrent use at 3.36 days per week; this is not a count of sessions. |
| long_video | money | Qualitative commercial-scale hypothesis only; no isolated long-form horizontal creative-video revenue series is selected. Total YouTube revenue would include other formats and content. |
| long_video | recognition | Hypothesis: distinctive essays, recurring creators, and memorable videos can persist, but recognition of a creator is not recognition of a particular work. |
| long_video | significance | An authored essay or vlog can develop a personally meaningful perspective; this potential is not a measured average across the proxy audience. |
| long_video | beliefs | Extended explanation and repeated creator contact may shape beliefs; factual instruction and artistic influence are not separated by the available evidence. |
| long_video | actions | A developed example may motivate a decision or project, but completed consequential action rates are unknown. |
| long_video | persistence | Rewatching, saved videos, and recurring creator contact may sustain influence; no comparative durable-effect rate is measured. |
| long_video | biography | A creator or body of videos may accompany a life period; attributing that role to a single video is uncertain. |
