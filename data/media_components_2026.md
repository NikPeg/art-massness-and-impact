# Media components across online formats, 2026

Separate from the [primary-format proxies](media_use_2026.md). No common cross-format component survey was selected. NA means unmeasured, not absent or zero.

| component | reach | time | frequency | inclusion | qualification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| music | NA | NA | NA | Standalone listening and audible music inside other media | Muted playback supplies no audible contact |
| images | NA | NA | NA | Standalone images and identifiable images displayed inside other media | A video view does not measure image-level viewers or visible time |
| text | NA | NA | NA | Reading standalone text and identifiable text displayed in other media | Text visibility does not establish reading or exposure to a complete source book |
| speech | NA | NA | NA | Audible speech in standalone audio and other media | Background playback does not establish attention or comprehension |

Record host format and component identity separately, with start/end, audible/visible status, prominence and uncertain coding. For example, a 30-second video can contain 30 seconds of music and 12 seconds of an identifiable image. These intervals do not total 72 seconds of elapsed attention.

Reach, time and days require within-component unions. Do not convert video view counts into unique component viewers or attribute an audiovisual outcome wholly to one component without an appropriate design. No component ranking is estimated.
