# Subjective score rationales

**Scope warning:** these earlier author profiles mix embedded music exposure with mostly standalone work formats. They are retained as author scenarios, not a harmonized comparison of either primary formats or media components. The [two-level framework](../research/01_scope_and_metrics.md#units-of-comparison) supersedes that comparative use. No replacement scores or cross-format component totals have been inferred.

Read with the [score table](subjective_scores_2026.md), [source register](../sources/source_register.md), and [limitations](../research/04_limitations.md). These are interpretive justifications, not measured effects. Numeric anchors can concern narrower populations or markets than the media category.

The underlying research questions are consolidated in [Project hypotheses](../research/06_hypotheses.md), especially H08–H19; their answers and evidence confidence are in [Findings](../results/01_findings.md#answers-to-the-project-hypotheses). The rationales below explain the revised online-content scores and do not constitute additional research findings.

| category | axis | rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| short_video | reach | Broad weekly reach in the online-adult survey; not a share of all humanity. |
| short_video | time | A substantial share of daily attention in GWI. |
| short_video | frequency | 4.26 days/week is the highest matched frequency proxy; revised to 9 rather than a ceiling of 10. Days are not sessions. |
| short_video | money | Reels has a large annualized pace; the landmark covers only Meta and is not realized annual revenue. |
| short_video | recognition | Hypothesis: individual clips often have shorter lives than songs or films, although templates and memes can persist. |
| short_video | life_impact | Author interpretation, 5/10: repeated clips and communities may shape habits and perspectives; sustained reflection varies widely. No supporting research for this exact score or comparative ordering has been found in the project review. |
| music | reach | 9 is a qualitative inclusive-online scenario: direct listening plus audible embedded music in vertical/horizontal video, online films, series and games. Streaming reach is 77.4%, not the union. No radio or offline audience is used; measured superiority over short video is unresolved. |
| music | time | 9 includes direct online listening and audible musical portions of online audiovisual works, deduplicated within music. The streaming-only reference is 301 minutes/week (score 6); IFPI total listening is excluded. The inclusive increment is not measured. |
| music | frequency | 9 includes days with audible music in online video, film, series and games as well as direct streams. The streaming-only reference is 3.10 days/week (score 7). Overlapping days cannot be added; radio supplies no uplift. |
| music | money | 7 is a low-confidence judgment of online music receipts, including attributable online licensing. Whole recorded-music income is contextual only; host video revenue is not all music revenue. |
| music | recognition | Repeated online listening, shared recordings and reuse in online video/film can sustain recognition of songs; this qualitative mechanism is not a measured comparative rate. |
| music | life_impact | Author interpretation, 8/10: emotional attachment, autobiographical memory, and identity may create lasting personal significance. No supporting research for this exact score or comparative ordering has been found in the project review. |
| film | reach | 7 is a low-confidence online-feature-viewing judgment. Cinema attendance and linear TV are excluded; the available streaming aggregate does not isolate films. |
| film | time | 5 is an online-only qualitative judgment of episodic long sessions; no feature-only matched minutes are available. |
| film | frequency | Retained at 5 for episodic online feature viewing; no film-only days/week measurement is selected. |
| film | money | 7 is a low-confidence online-film monetization judgment; box office is not an anchor, and shared streaming revenue cannot be allocated from the selected data. |
| film | recognition | Established stories, characters, scenes, and quotations can remain recognizable. |
| film | life_impact | Author interpretation, 8/10: concentrated narrative and memorable characters may leave lasting perspectives and personal reference points. No supporting research for this exact score or comparative ordering has been found in the project review. |
| series | reach | 72.0% TV-streaming reach supports a conservative 7; films and other programs remain included in this proxy. Linear TV is excluded. |
| series | time | 308 streaming minutes/week supports 6, close to music streaming and long online video; the series-only share is unknown. |
| series | frequency | 2.77 TV-streaming days/week supports a lower judgment of 6; this is not a series-only estimate. |
| series | money | 8 is an online-series commercial-scale hypothesis; total Netflix revenue includes films and is no longer a numeric anchor. |
| series | recognition | Long-lived characters and franchises can sustain recognition. |
| series | life_impact | Author interpretation, 8/10: repeated contact with characters and stories may shape identity and social perspectives. No supporting research for this exact score or comparative ordering has been found in the project review. |
| books | reach | 4 is a low-confidence digital-fiction audience judgment, revised after excluding printed-book readers. No matched digital-only reach is selected; the reduction is not an empirical estimate. |
| books | time | 4 is a low-confidence digital-reading judgment; all-format US leisure reading no longer anchors it. No digital-only minutes are selected. |
| books | frequency | 5 is a low-confidence digital-reading habit judgment; no matched digital-fiction days/week are selected. |
| books | money | 5 is a low-confidence digital-fiction monetization judgment. Print-inclusive US fiction publisher revenue no longer anchors the rating. |
| books | recognition | Online availability, digital reissues and adaptations may extend recognition of digitally distributed texts; physical sales are not rating support. |
| books | life_impact | Author interpretation, 9/10: sustained reading and revisiting ideas may support reflection and major choices; this high score reflects author bias, not demonstrated superiority. No supporting research for this exact score or comparative ordering has been found in the project review. |
| games | reach | 74.4% all-device gaming among online adults informs 7; this proxy does not isolate downloaded/streamed games or exclude physical distribution. |
| games | time | GWI combines mobile, PC, and console gaming time. |
| games | frequency | 7 is a qualitative judgment informed by 2.80 mobile and 1.73 PC/console days/week. Their union is unknown and the two values are not added. |
| games | money | 9 is a qualitative online-distributed-game commercial-scale judgment. The whole Newzoo market is not an isolated online-distribution numeric anchor. |
| games | recognition | Characters, worlds, and franchises can become durable cultural references. |

| long_video | reach | 81.2% weekly proxy reach is below short video (88.5%); revised to 8. The horizontal-video subset remains unidentified. |
| long_video | time | 299 minutes/week is below short video and games; revised to 6, tied with near-equal streaming music and TV-streaming proxies. |
| long_video | frequency | 3.36 days/week supports a lower judgment than short video; revised to 7, with the broader-proxy caveat. |
| long_video | money | Qualitative commercial-scale hypothesis only; no isolated long-form horizontal online-video revenue series is selected. Total YouTube revenue would include other formats and content. |
| long_video | recognition | Hypothesis: distinctive essays, recurring creators, and memorable videos can persist, but recognition of a creator is not recognition of a particular work. |
| long_video | life_impact | Author interpretation, 8/10: developed explanations and repeated creator contact may support reflection and consequential choices. No supporting research for this exact score or comparative ordering has been found in the project review. |
| pictures | reach | Hypothesis of broad circulation across feeds and messaging; platform users cannot be counted as picture viewers. No matched reach estimate. |
| pictures | time | Brief encounters and deliberate viewing coexist. A middle score is an unmeasured model choice; feed minutes cannot isolate pictures. |
| pictures | frequency | Hypothesis of repeated daily encounters in mixed feeds; no picture-only days/week observation was selected. |
| pictures | money | Qualitative placeholder for distributed licensing, commissions, and platform activity; no comparable category revenue or numeric anchor. |
| pictures | recognition | Recognizable images and templates may persist. PICMEM26 links predicted memorability to commenting, not cross-generational cultural recognition. |
| pictures | life_impact | Author interpretation, 6/10: salient images may shape memory, identity, and social perspectives; depth of engagement varies widely. No supporting research for this exact score or comparative ordering has been found in the project review. |

## Pictures: basis and uncertainty

The pictures profile is low-confidence and heterogeneous. All scores are author judgments; the linked research does not establish a cross-medium ranking.
