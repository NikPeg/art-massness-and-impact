RADAR DIAGRAMS: READING GUIDE

Alphabetical order of English category names, left to right, then top to bottom.
Subjective author ratings from 0 to 10, not measured effects. The project review
has found no research establishing the exact Life impact scores or format order.
Area does not represent an overall ranking; a one-point gap is not interpreted.

Axes clockwise from the top:
Reach: audience reach.
Time: consumption time.
Frequency: frequency of use.
Money: commercial scale.
Recognition: cultural recognition.
Life impact: potential lasting influence on views, habits and decisions.

Circle: numeric source anchor. Hollow diamond: qualitative rationale.
Both types of ratings are subjective. Categories and audiences overlap.
Music: blue includes direct and embedded online contact;
orange dashed is streaming only (8/6/7), not a measured increase.
Overlaps within music require deduplication;
music and video minutes must not be summed as independent time shares.
