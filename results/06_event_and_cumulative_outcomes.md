# Major events and everyday or cumulative changes: separate results

This extension answers the concern that event-centered outcomes privilege conspicuous biographical changes. It separates two equally relevant outcome families; neither substitutes for the other. The [study-by-study appraisal](../research/08_cumulative_media_effects.md) records designs, findings, confidence, retrieval limits and original sources.

## Major events

The evidence still does not supply comparable attributable rates of completed moves, job changes or relationship decisions across the eight media. Existing bounded narrative/behavioral cases remain in the [literature review](../research/02_literature_review.md); they do not establish a universal ranking. This lack of event-level evidence says nothing by itself about the presence or absence of everyday influence.

## Everyday and cumulative changes

Image-exposure evidence is assigned to **pictures**, one of the project's eight categories. Instagram identifies where the images were delivered, not the scope of the outcome claim. Mixed-platform studies remain separate evidence that cannot identify which creative-content category contributed. See the [evidence-to-category mapping](../research/08_cumulative_media_effects.md#which-project-categories-the-evidence-concerns).

These results justify broadening the operational definition of influence. They do not show that all brief encounters accumulate, that every change persists, or that one medium has more total life impact. A repeated benefit or harm during exposure and a lasting change after exposure are both relevant but different findings. Lack of a major event cannot be coded as zero everyday impact.

The [study proposal](03_next_study.md#outcomes) now specifies separate event and everyday/cumulative outputs, baseline and repeated measures, and follow-up persistence. Life impact remains unassessed on the radar: this heterogeneous literature provides no calibrated numerical comparison. No consumption or money scores or empirical chart inputs change in this extension.
