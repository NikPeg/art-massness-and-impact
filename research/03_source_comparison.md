# Comparison of sources and numbers

## Apparent discrepancies

| Numbers encountered | Why they are not contradictory measurements of the same thing |
| --- | --- |
| Music: 20.7 hours/week, IFPI 2023; music streaming: 5 hours 1 minute/week, GWI 2026 | Total listening versus one delivery channel; different samples and years |
| Short video: 6 hours 42 minutes/week, July 2025 report; 7 hours 6 minutes/week, 2026 snapshot | Different survey/report vintages; a clean growth rate is not established |
| Recorded music in 2021: $25.9 billion in the original release; $23.7 billion in the selected IFPI 2026 restatement | Revisions and exchange-rate/coverage treatment; the data table uses the restated series consistently |
| Games in 2025: $188.8 billion forecast in the 2025 report; $201.6 billion in the June 2026 update | Forecast versus later revised estimate; not evidence of growth between two calendar years |
| US publishing in 2025: $33.4 billion total; $21.7 billion consumer books; $12.5 billion selected fiction categories | Nested scopes, not rival estimates of one market |
| Netflix 2025: over 95 billion viewing hours in the first half and 96 billion in the second | Different half-years, both service-wide; neither isolates series |
| Reels: over $50 billion annualized pace; recorded music: $31.7 billion annual revenue | Timing and accounting boundaries differ; neither measures all media activity |

These comparisons are grounded in the linked source register and the sources below. [Original IFPI 2021 revenue release](https://www.ifpi.org/ifpi-global-music-report-global-recorded-music-revenues-grew-18-5-in-2021/) supplies the superseded music number; it is not used in the selected historical data.

## International survey versus time-use diary

GWI's 2026 figures describe internet users aged 16+ in its covered countries. BLS ATUS describes US residents aged 15+ and records the main activity in a time diary. ATUS does not add simultaneous background listening. Their levels must not be combined into a global ranking. [GWI report](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-global-overview-report); [ATUS definitions and tables](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/atus.t11A.htm).

The GWI report published in October 2026 is titled **Digital 2027**. Conversely, **Digital 2026** was published in October 2025. Report names are not observation dates. GWI also changed media-use questions and removed Russia. The selected 2026 snapshot is not connected to older snapshots as a continuous series.

## Music: different boundaries

IFPI's 2023 total listening measure includes more listening methods than GWI's music-streaming category. The larger IFPI estimate cannot establish growth or leadership against the 2026 streaming estimate. Differences reflect time, geography, age, and scope. [IFPI listening study](https://www.ifpi.org/ifpis-global-study-finds-were-listening-to-more-music-in-more-ways-than-ever/).

The financial series uses IFPI 2026's restated historical values reproduced in UMG's annual report. It does not splice older press releases together. Old releases can differ because IFPI restates exchange rates and historical coverage. Concert income remains outside recorded-music revenue. [UMG report, p. 15](https://assets.ctfassets.net/e66ejtqbaazg/7HObJrt5UDQfjjfKYN454B/201bf057190c5ed4ee185b04a34ec095/UMG_2025_Annual_Report.pdf).

## Video games: reporting-vintage break

The selected Newzoo 2025 report contains revised estimates for 2022–2024. Its 2025 value was a forecast and is not used as an actual result. The separate June 2026 article supplies an updated 2025 estimate. These vintages remain separately labeled in the data table. Earlier figures encountered in searches, such as 2021 at about $192.7 billion, were excluded from the selected data because a consistent series was not established. [2025 report mirror](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025_Newzoo_Free_Global_Games_Market_Report.pdf); [2026 update](https://newzoo.com/articles/global-games-market-2025).

These are financial landmarks, retained in the [source table](../data/annual_indicators.md). The common financial chart has been removed because a shared dollar axis visually implies comparable commercial scale despite incompatible market, geographic, and accounting boundaries. Gower Street's annual estimates also come from different vintages and exchange-rate contexts; slopes should not be interpreted as clean real growth.

## Long video: source category versus study category

GWI reports 81.2% weekly reach, 299 minutes per week, and 3.36 days per week for long online video. The daily-time chart divides 299 by seven (42.71 minutes). These measure the source-defined long-online-video category; a horizontal-only subgroup is not isolated. Short video has higher observed reach (88.5%), weekly time (426 minutes), and frequency (4.26 days) in this snapshot. This is a descriptive source-category comparison, not a causal influence estimate. [GWI / DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-global-overview-report).

Do not subtract Shorts totals from all-YouTube totals to estimate this category: definitions, units, and populations may differ, and the remainder still contains films, music, news, and other excluded material. No financial value or five-year consumption series is inferred from that subtraction.

| --- | --- |
| Video start | Complete attentive viewing |
| Paid membership | Unique individual viewer |
| Book sale | Completed reading |
| Hours on Netflix | Hours of series only |
| Meaningfulness rating | Completed life-changing action |
| Trial sign-up click | Actual clinical-trial participation |
| Association with reading | Causal effect of a particular book |

## Pictures: platform activity, creation, and consumption

Instagram/Pinterest/Reddit audience or revenue is not pictures-only activity. Mixed feeds include video, text, advertising, and messaging; gallery, licensing, and physical-image markets have other boundaries. No comparable picture-only financial series or 2026 consumption observation was selected, so the common figures display explicit gaps.

PICMEM26 measures a correlation with comments; PICSHARE25 measures photo-related recall. These outcomes cannot substitute for days/week, person-hours, common cultural recognition, or major life changes.

## Primary formats versus media components

The [format snapshot](../data/media_use_2026.md) and [component inventory](../data/media_components_2026.md) answer separate questions. Streaming music must not be expanded with soundtracks while images remains restricted to image posts. A painting in a vertical video counts in the image component just as audible music counts in the music component. Literary text, lyrics and performance are eligible across hosts under the same rule. Existing aggregate surveys do not measure these unions. In particular, a standalone-picture gap does not imply zero image contact, and a video view does not measure the painting's unique audience or attentive duration. A filmed excerpt is not a full source-work exposure; adaptations do not establish source-book readership.
