# Methods and reproduction

## Scope and comparison units

The project is a research dossier with a moderate targeted literature review on eight categories of internet-distributed media content: short-form video, long-form horizontal creator video, music, feature films, series, books, video games and pictures. These categories overlap; they are not exclusive shares of a common market or attention budget. Definitions and inclusion rules are in [scope and measurement](01_scope_and_metrics.md).

Keep reach, consumption time, frequency, commercial scale, cultural recognition and life influence distinct. Life influence has two separately reported outcome families: major events and everyday/cumulative changes. Within everyday outcomes, distinguish immediate responses, repeated responses, during-exposure trajectories and persistence after exposure changes or ends. No common cross-medium life-impact index is estimated.

For comparisons of influence, distinguish equal attentive time, complete works and repeated practices over a common period. Creator relationships and community participation are separate exposure units. A single clip is not equivalent to years of participation in a community. The [proposed primary study](../results/03_next_study.md) specifies measurements needed for these comparisons; that study has not been conducted.

## Evidence selection and literature-review procedure

The literature component is a moderate targeted review. A corrective [matched search](../sources/balanced_review_search_2026.md) now applies affect/well-being, beliefs/attitudes/social cognition, and behavior/follow-up blocks to all eight formats. The [evidence matrix](09_balanced_literature_review.md) separates direct online evidence, provisional abstracts, mismatched context and unresolved cells. Original reports and papers remain the evidence sources; the search label does not imply moderate confidence in every conclusion.

The [initial record](../sources/moderate_review_search_2026.md) preserves seven retained query records and their decisions. The new stage records 32 equally allocated queries and 18 additional paper-retrieval/gap queries, with exact strings, access levels and decisions. It does not retroactively make the original search symmetric. Other targeted work remains in the [research log](../sources/research_log.md), including the [everyday/cumulative appraisal](08_cumulative_media_effects.md). No complete retrospective hit census is claimed.

This bounded search procedure is repeatable, but web rankings may change and a complete export of returned hits is unavailable. There was no systematic database search, exhaustive citation chasing, independent screening or formal study-level risk-of-bias assessment. Original full-text, abstract-only and discovery-only sources remain distinguishable in the [source register](../sources/source_register.md). No effect-direction filter is used for the documented search. Missing or unretrieved evidence is not evidence of no effect.

## Hypothesis-answer status and scope fit

Answer status measures correspondence to the original question; confidence measures the strength of evidence for the stated answer. Apply status first, without rewriting the question to fit the available source. All hypotheses inherit the project's internet-distributed media-content scope, including questions whose wording does not repeat it.

For every question, check: (1) content category and online distribution; (2) exposure unit and dose (work, feed, creator or community); (3) population and geography; (4) comparator and coverage of the comparison set; (5) outcome and follow-up; and (6) whether the design supports the requested descriptive, associative or causal inference. The [findings table](../results/01_findings.md#answers-to-the-project-hypotheses) records the decisive mismatch for every hypothesis.

| Status | Decision rule |
| --- | --- |
| Substantive answer | Evidence addresses the original in-scope claim, its required comparator, outcomes and timescale, without narrowing a material element. For a causal question, the design must support the causal contrast. A bounded existence claim can be answered by an eligible case, but an offline case cannot answer an online existence claim. |
| Partial answer | Evidence establishes an explicit subquestion, narrower population, outcome component or operational proxy. State the bounded result and the missing part. A descriptive proxy can answer a proxy comparison only; it does not establish the original category-level or causal conclusion. |
| No answer from the available evidence | No eligible evidence establishes the original claim or an informative bounded subquestion. Evidence about another category, offline delivery, an untested mechanism or a necessary-but-insufficient intermediate step remains context. Do not assign directional confidence to the original hypothesis. |

A reduced geographical scope yields a partial answer, even when its survey contrast is precisely reported. A related-medium mechanism alone yields no answer to a medium-specific causal question. For compound questions, evidence for an actual outcome component may yield a partial answer; evidence for a different outcome does not. Missing evidence is not rejection. Non-significance is not equivalence, and failure to show a persuasion advantage does not establish an audience-attraction mechanism.

There is no separate Tentative status: uncertainty in a bounded supported result belongs in its confidence/reason, while a plausible but untested explanation remains a hypothesis under No answer. Apply the same rules to positive and negative claims. An empty Substantive category is allowed; statuses do not need to contain a predetermined number of answers. This is an applicability audit of the selected evidence, not a new systematic review or a validation of source data.

## Confidence in conclusions and search coverage

Confidence attaches to the exact claim and population, not to a medium as a whole. Consider design quality and causal identification, directness of exposure/comparator/outcome/follow-up, consistency across relevant evidence, and precision where checked. High/Moderate/Low labels are informal reasoned assessments, not scores, calibrated probabilities or a completed standardized certainty framework. High confidence in a reported survey observation does not imply high confidence in a broad causal comparison.

Use Not assigned when no comparable body supports the requested conclusion. Abstract-only evidence is provisional, with unverified details stated; a causal conclusion cannot be upgraded from an abstract alone. Report search coverage separately, including languages, retrieval failures and unsearched databases. A non-significant result does not establish a negligible effect without considering precision. Studies sharing participants, and reviews containing the same primary studies, are not independent replications.

## Empirical inputs and transformations

Markdown tables in `data/` are the authoritative script inputs. `NA` means missing, not zero. Incompatible populations and reporting vintages remain separate; no five-year series is interpolated or inferred from platform totals.

| Input | Treatment | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [2026 media use](../data/media_use_2026.md) | Convert reported weekly hours/minutes to weekly minutes, then divide by seven for a daily mean; frequency stays days per week | International online-adult survey categories do not consistently isolate all eight project categories or online distribution |
| [Annual context](../data/annual_indicators.md) | Store annual dollars in billions; sum the two AAP fiction categories; multiply reported BLS hours by 60 for the US time-use figure | Market totals, company revenue, box office and annualized revenue pace are incompatible; financial values appear in tables without a common chart |

Whole-market revenues, print-inclusive publishing, cinema attendance, broad listening and US diaries are contextual evidence, not matched online-only observations. Missing online-only measures remain gaps. Consumer participation, original creation, remixing and redistribution are distinct activities.

## Two comparison levels

Use [the symmetric two-level scope](01_scope_and_metrics.md#units-of-comparison): primary work formats and media components across those formats. The [snapshot](../data/media_use_2026.md) preserves broad source-format proxies; the [component inventory](../data/media_components_2026.md) contains missing cross-format totals. Do not inflate the streaming-music row with soundtracks or exclude images merely because it is presented in video. Code one host exposure and linked component intervals, identities, modality and prominence. Deduplicate separately within each level and component; do not add component-minutes as exclusive attention or infer effects from presence.

## Category-specific measurement

**Long horizontal video.** The empirical category follows the broad source definition of long online video. Landscape orientation and a ten-minute cutoff are optional future subgroups, not eligibility requirements for the aggregate comparison. Feature films and scripted series have separate primary categories. GWI long online video is a broader reach/time/frequency proxy that does not verify these inclusion rules. All five financial observations for this category are missing. Creator attachment, genre and duration are not causally separated by the available aggregate observations.

**Pictures.** Online photography, illustration, drawings, collages and image memes follow the scope rules. No matched picture-only observation supplies reach, time, frequency or revenue for the common tables. Empirical values remain missing; all six radar data markers are qualitative. Gallery experience, memory, news attention and creator-only main-type shares answer different questions and do not calibrate the radar.

**Music.** Comparison A covers standalone audio works, with streaming as its retained source proxy. Comparison B covers both standalone and embedded music symmetrically with the other media components. The earlier subjective music profile covers direct listening and embedded music and therefore mixes comparison levels with the other profiles; it is retained as an earlier author scenario, not a harmonized comparison. Combined reach, time and days require within-music unions, not summed platform audiences. The [music scenario table](../author-interpretations/music_online_scenarios.md) provides a streaming-only reference, drawn as a dashed segment on the first three radar axes. This is a difference in exposure definition, not a measured uplift. The literature does not causally isolate musical contributions from the host audiovisual work.


## Subjective radar profiles

All eight profiles have the same six axes in order: reach, time, frequency, money, cultural recognition and general Life impact. Each axis has a subjective 0–10 author score, giving 48 scores and 48 rationale entries. The [score table](../author-interpretations/subjective_scores_2026.md) and [rationales](../author-interpretations/score_rationales.md) define the current inputs. Profiles are closed and lightly filled; the overview and gallery follow alphabetical English category names, without area ranking or a composite total.

The radius is linear on the author scale. No normalization against observed consumption or revenue, calibrated evidence-to-score formula, independent assessor procedure or empirical validation of the point differences is claimed. A filled circle means a numerical source informed a judgment; a hollow diamond means qualitative reasoning only. Both remain subjective scores. The general Life impact score represents author interpretation of potential lasting influence on an engaged person, not a measured effect or an established comparative ordering. It is not an average of detailed research outcomes. The illustrative profiles are separate from the literature-based findings.

## Figure generation and reproduction

Run from the project root with Python 3.9 or newer:

```sh
python3 scripts/build_figures.py
python3 scripts/validate.py
```

The standard-library generator reads local tables and writes 13 standalone SVGs, adjacent TXT annotations and two HTML galleries (`figures/index.html` for empirical/context charts and `author-interpretations/index.html` for author interpretations): eight individual radars, one eight-panel overview, four consumption-context figures. No network request, external chart library or empirical re-estimation occurs during the build. SVG figures are in English; the author-interpretation gallery, its README and radar reading guide are in Russian. The [script notes](../scripts/README.md) describe output and marker conventions.

Empirical SVGs include precise source-category labels, explicit missing observations, population, period, units, source and essential scope limits directly in the image. TXT sidecars provide extended context rather than carrying the only explanation. Snapshot categories and the US diary activities have separate labels reflecting their different measurements.

## Validation and maintenance

The validator checks input categories and counts, selected numerical anchors, missing values, score bounds, rationale coverage, music-scenario consistency, SVG structure, overview panels, accessibility IDs, label bounds, TXT consistency, local file links and figure language. The [validation record](../results/04_validation.md) states current checks and limits. These checks establish packaging and internal consistency, not pixel-perfect layout, source accuracy, search completeness or scientific validity of author judgments.

For an empirical input change, update its table, source and method note, rebuild affected outputs and validate. For an author score change, update its rationale and audit as well. Do not fill a missing empirical observation with an opinion. The [further-work plan](../FURTHER_WORK.md) specifies the protocol, comprehensive search, independent appraisal and synthesis required for a full systematic review.

Historical implementation notes are kept in [the method archive](../sources/method_history.md) and [research log](../sources/research_log.md), separately from this description of the current methods.
