# Limitations

## Literature search coverage and confidence

The literature review is a moderate targeted review, corrected on 9 November 2026 with [matched outcome queries](../sources/balanced_review_search_2026.md) and a [common evidence matrix](09_balanced_literature_review.md). It is not a systematic database review: English-language web discovery, ranking, selective follow-up and variable full-text access can omit positive, null or conflicting studies. There was no independent second screener, exhaustive citation search, complete hit export or formal study-level risk-of-bias assessment.

**Outcome-selection bias is separate from search completeness.** The original queries assigned social cognition to reading, prosocial behavior to music/games and well-being to pictures. Any resulting comparative impression could reflect the queries. The matched stage corrects that allocation, but cannot undo unequal indexing, retrieval access or research availability. Additional gap searches were unequal and are disclosed separately. No directional conclusion follows from an empty matrix cell.

Coverage and appraisal depth differ across categories. Several candidates are abstract-only; long-video creator studies do not verify the duration/orientation or content-type criteria. Outcomes range from cognition and intentions to observed behavior and delayed responses. This evidence cannot support a cross-medium life-impact ranking. “Not found in the documented search” is not evidence that a study does not exist, and missing evidence is not evidence of no effect. Non-significant findings also do not by themselves establish a practically negligible effect.

Confidence labels describe a specified conclusion using available design quality, directness, consistency and precision. They are informal narrative assessments, not calibrated probabilities or a completed standardized certainty appraisal. Search completeness is a separate limitation and cannot be repaired by raising or lowering a confidence label. The [further-work plan](../FURTHER_WORK.md) sets out the protocol, database search, independent screening, bias appraisal and synthesis needed for a full review.

## Overlapping media formats

Music, images, text and performance can be present across short/long video, online films, series, books and games. A painting shown in a video is image contact, rather than only a video-format observation. This is a substantive part of online musical exposure, not measurement noise. A minute of a music-driven video can be both video exposure and music exposure. Counting both is legitimate for separate component-contact questions; adding them into a person's exclusive time budget double-counts the minute. Books also circulate through adaptations. The same intellectual property can generate multiple revenues and audiences.

## Long horizontal video limitations

- **Source definition:** GWI reports long online video without verifying a common duration cutoff or aspect ratio. Findings use that broad category; a horizontal-only subgroup remains unmeasured.
- **Arbitrary boundaries:** 10 minutes is an operational proposal. Landscape videos can be short; long videos can be vertical. Documentary films, episodic vlogs, video podcasts and music performances require primary-category rules and secondary tags. Classification needs independent coders and agreement checks before population estimates.
- **More overlap:** a viewer can encounter the same creator or work in a Short, a full video, a podcast, and a film. Shorts may direct people to longer works. Audience shares and time cannot be added without person-level de-duplication; influence cannot be attributed to length from these aggregates.
- **Mixed mechanisms:** essays, vlogs, performances, explainers, and interviews vary in narrative, factual information, and creator relationships. The existing narrative-persuasion literature does not validate an effect estimate for this category.
- **Dose and creator confounding:** a longer work has more exposure time, while repeated contact with a creator or community may explain significance. Duration, genre, prior interest, attention, and creator attachment need separate measurement. Autoplay, skipped segments, playback speed, and background listening further separate reported minutes from attentive exposure.
- **Financial and historical gaps:** no isolated 2021–2025 revenue or matched consumption series is selected for long horizontal video. Platform totals mix formats and revenue streams; sponsorship, memberships, and off-platform income can overlap. These gaps prevent a complete market comparison.
- **Subjective judgments:** the package contains 48 author scores across eight profiles and six axes, including general Life impact. Three consumption anchors are broader proxies and the money rating is qualitative. The apparent position of long video relative to books, films, or short video is an author hypothesis, not new empirical evidence about life change.

## Symmetry of format and component comparisons

Earlier author profiles included embedded soundtracks in music while usually treating pictures as standalone posts and books as digital reading. That construction mixed levels and could make music appear broader by definition. The current framework separates [primary formats and media components](01_scope_and_metrics.md#units-of-comparison). Images in a vertical video, film or game is eligible under the same component rule as an embedded soundtrack; text and speech are also coded across hosts. Digital-only distribution applies to all categories. The existing aggregate sources do not implement this coding and cannot supply cross-format component unions or a component ranking. Earlier subjective profiles are retained with a mixed-scope warning; their scores have not been recalibrated as either of the new comparisons. Presence does not establish attentive viewing or causal contribution.

## Online-distribution scope

The target is internet-distributed media content. Broad listening, print-inclusive books, cinema receipts and US time diaries do not provide numerical anchors for the online-content radar judgments. Contextual tables are retained with their original boundaries. Missing online-only measurements remain missing. The earlier inclusive music profile is a qualitative scenario covering direct and embedded audible contact, with a separate streaming-only reference. It is not a valid harmonized comparison against standalone-format profiles; the two-level framework supersedes this use. Its reach, time and frequency have hollow markers because no measured union is available. Neither its exact uplift nor its ordering against short video is identified. Film, digital books, pictures and online-only revenues remain particularly uncertain; lowering an author rating does not measure the digital share of a broader market.

## Denominators and geographic coverage

The empirical materials mix international online-adult surveys, US population diaries, global industry estimates. No single target population is represented by all eight categories. Children, offline audiences, and countries excluded from particular surveys can change the ordering.

## Incomplete and changing time series

Five complete years means 2021–2025. The 2026 survey is a separate partial-year snapshot. Historical survey redesigns, revised market estimates, exchange rates, pandemic closures prevent simple trend comparisons. Missing observations remain gaps. No yearly series of life-changing influence is fabricated.

## Limits of the growth evidence

The [growth appraisal](08_growth_evidence_search.md) and [inspected GMI26 chart](../data/media_growth_h1_2026.md) support moderate confidence in bounded service growth and the reported 12-category percentage-growth ranking. Chart 573622 explicitly shows H1 2026, ages 16–64, short video +5.2%, long video +1.4% and streaming +1.3%. Its baseline period is not printed; author discussion identifies year-on-year growth. Country weights, matched-wave composition and uncertainty remain unaudited. H04/H05 retain partial status: vertical/horizontal orientation, digital books and standalone pictures are not isolated, and streaming mixes films/series/other programmes. GMI26 and DataReportal share GWI data and are not independent replications. Ofcom/Ipsos iris covers whole UK services; Qustodio covers selected monitored children. Older +5.97% and +0.67% cross-release arithmetic remains unsuitable; unaudited CNNIC/QuestMobile and Sensor Tower leads do not increase confidence.

## Selection, attribution, and causality

People may select books or videos because they are already considering a decision. A work can be a catalyst, a justification, a source of language, or one influence among many. Retrospective statements do not isolate its causal contribution. An intervention's effect on a narrow behavior cannot be assumed to generalize to migration, career changes, or relationships.

## Event-centered outcomes and cumulative change

An event-only definition would undercount ordinary changes in mood, self-esteem, habits, social belonging and perceived norms, especially for music, images and feeds. The [measurement definition](01_scope_and_metrics.md#what-life-changing-would-require) requires separate event and everyday/cumulative outputs; the [cumulative-effects review](08_cumulative_media_effects.md) appraises relevant evidence. A lack of major events is not evidence of no everyday impact.

Separate outcome families do not close the evidence gap. Repeated state reactions are not automatically persistent changes; a long observation period is not itself evidence of accumulation. Temporal aggregation, reverse selection, concurrent offline experiences and person-specific differences can change associations. Null average trajectories and unsuccessful regulation must remain visible. Much of the online evidence measures entire platforms or messaging rather than media content; selected music data do not isolate streaming or embedded soundtracks. The norm-change example uses offline radio and remains contextual only. Evidence is insufficient for a calibrated eight-medium comparison of cumulative life influence.

## Contact versus cumulative environment

One book versus one 30-second clip compares different doses. A year of reading versus a year of using a feed asks a different question. Repetition may make individually minor contacts consequential. This dossier does not estimate exposure-response functions or cumulative causal effects. The cumulative-effects review distinguishes observed repeated-measure patterns from causal accumulation and from persistence after exposure; it includes null and mixed findings.

## Background exposure versus attention

Listening can occur while working or traveling. Watching a film can involve concentrated attention. These experiences differ in attention without one being inherently more valuable. No validated attention-weighting factor is applied.

## What the radar scores are

All 48 numeric radar values are subjective judgments. Life impact is subjective author interpretation of potential lasting influence on an engaged person. Research supporting these exact scores or their comparative ordering has not been found in the project review. No calibrated procedure translates evidence into these judgments. A numeric anchor informs a judgment but is not a mathematical conversion. Broad-format judgments are sometimes informed by narrower proxies. The score rationale explicitly identifies those proxies.

The model shows aggregate reach, consumption, market scale, and cultural recognition; general life impact reflects author interpretation of potential influence on an engaged person. It is a profile, not a latent scientific index. The suggested ±2-point latitude is a qualitative caution, not a confidence interval, expert-consensus range, or computed uncertainty estimate.

## Bias in the impact ratings

The former detailed impact scores implied an unsupported comparative advantage, particularly for books over short video. Those axes and scores have been removed. Their replacement, general Life impact, now has author scores at the user's request. There is no calibrated evidence-to-score procedure or matched comparison. The higher book score is an author assumption and may reflect bias. No former impact scores were averaged or transferred. Genres and individual works vary greatly within each category.

## Radar geometry

Polygon area depends on axis order and on an arbitrary ordinal scale. Adjacent dimensions are correlated. The current profiles are closed and lightly filled and are displayed in alphabetical category order. Their filled areas do not establish overall superiority. No area ranking or sum is computed. The axis order and scale are held constant only to make profile differences readable.

## Evidence and retrieval limits

This is a targeted review without preregistration or exhaustive screening. Some material was available as a publisher abstract, indexed search extract, or a third-party mirror of an original report. Public pages may change. No full source archive, survey microdata, independent re-analysis, or peer review is included. Retrieval status is documented in the source register.

**Figure reproducibility is stronger than source-data reproducibility.** The scripts deterministically regenerate visualizations from the transcribed Markdown tables, but this does not reproduce or independently verify the extraction of those numbers from the original sources. Some evidence is retained only through mutable web pages, search extracts, or materials without archived copies. Available original tables, exact supporting excerpts, and source-version dates have not been systematically preserved. A retrieval date alone does not identify the exact content or version consulted. Consequently, a reader can reproduce the visualization of the transcribed values more confidently than repeat their extraction and check their original context. The [source register](../sources/source_register.md) records access and use, but is not a complete versioned source archive.

**Priority for further work: high, ahead of additional presentation and styling.** Preserving available source tables, exact supporting excerpts and identifiable source versions, and checking the extraction against them, takes priority over further visual polish. A successful build or validation run establishes consistency of the local inputs and outputs within the checks performed; it does not establish that the values were correctly extracted or that the underlying evidence is valid. This priority identifies outstanding work, not an archive or extraction audit already completed.

## Pictures-specific limitations

Photography, illustration, image memes, and digital reproductions have different purposes and barriers. The category is a working umbrella. Low technical entry barriers for a phone photograph do not establish equal access to professional creation or a sense that an media form is “for people like me.” No common belonging measure was selected.

Attention, machine-predicted memorability, recall after sharing, curated-gallery well-being, and major life changes are distinct outcomes. A one-image exposure, a carousel, and months in a mixed feed are different doses. AI-assisted production and reposting need separate coding. The all-hollow pictures radar contains ten uncertain author judgments; none of its consumption or revenue values has a matched numerical anchor. The later online-scope revision reviews all eight profiles; pictures retain their existing online-only judgments. No measured superiority of pictures over another category is claimed.
