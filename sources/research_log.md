## Search strategy

Targeted web searches covered international media time and reach; short-video counting definitions; recorded-music revenue and listening; game market estimates; worldwide box office; Netflix financial/viewing disclosures; book publishing; narrative persuasion; meaningful entertainment; and transformative reading.

Primary industry organizations, company filings, official time-use statistics, and published research were preferred. Search-engine dates were not treated as observation dates. Original chart footnotes were checked where article prose used less precise labels.

## Corrections and interpretation safeguards

- An early conversational arithmetic example contained a self-correction from “half-hour” to “half-minute.” The dossier uses the correct 30-second example throughout.
- GWI frequency is days per week, not number of viewing sessions.
- Total music and music streaming are different measures.
- A sign-up click in the short-video study is not completed clinical-trial participation.
- The 2026 and 2027 report titles refer to publication products, not necessarily the year of observation.
- The user-requested radar scores are not conclusions established by the cited papers.

## Unresolved evidence gaps

No selected public dataset provides matched global 2021–2025 observations across all seven categories for unique audience, exclusive person-hours, major attributed actions, durable effects, or biographical importance. This is a limitation of this targeted search, not proof that no relevant dataset exists anywhere.
