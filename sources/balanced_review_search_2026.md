# Matched literature search, 9 November 2026

## Purpose and boundary

This is the corrective stage of the project's **moderate targeted literature review**. The earlier [search record](moderate_review_search_2026.md) used different outcomes for different categories. That can bias comparative impressions independently of incomplete database coverage. The new stage applies the same outcome blocks to every category and reports the remaining empty cells. Equal search effort does not imply equal evidence, and this is not a systematic review or a ranking of media.

Search used the general web-search tool in English, without a tool date filter. Eligibility cutoff: 9 November 2026. Original journal pages, author/institutional repositories and original indexed abstracts were followed. No direct PsycINFO/Scopus/Web of Science search, independent screening, exhaustive citation chasing, or formal risk-of-bias assessment was completed. These limits apply even when full text was retrieved.

## Common questions and execution

Three blocks were run for each of the eight format strings below, giving **24 matched queries**. The exact submitted query is the format string, one space, and the block suffix (no extra parentheses). A second pass used the eight exact synonym queries below, giving **32 queries with equal allocation across categories**. The synonym pass broadened retrieval after the first pass; neither stage was preregistered. Clinical/educational content was not selectively allowed for one format and forbidden for another: it must be labeled as purpose-designed content, with no transfer to typical consumption.

## Inclusion and removal rules

Retain studies that report a defined exposure, comparator or longitudinal contrast, a relevant A/B/C outcome, and usable methods/results. Code online delivery, exposure dose, comparator, sample, outcome instrument/type and timing separately. Full-text inspection means the relevant sections were read, not a formal bias audit. Abstract-level records remain provisional. Retain null and mixed findings. Do not add primary samples to pooled reviews as independent replications.

Remove unappraised leads and endpoints outside the current life-influence comparison from the main synthesis; preserve their provenance and rejection reasons here or in the earlier register. Attention, recall and platform circulation can remain relevant to other project questions, but cannot fill an influence-outcome cell. Offline studies remain labeled mechanism context, regardless of medium or effect direction.

## Earlier material removed from the main influence synthesis

- Book-longevity associations, the unappraised transformative-reading dissertation and the UCL vocabulary lead: not appraised evidence for the shared A/B/C contrast.
- The short-video prospective-memory lead and PICNEWS24: cognition/attention endpoints do not establish the selected life-influence outcomes.
- PICSHARE25 and PICMEM26: recall after sharing and predicted image circulation do not establish affect, attitudes or completed action caused by reception.
- Unappraised advertising and conference leads: no usable primary appraisal for this synthesis.

Their links remain in earlier source records; removal from this synthesis does not assert that the studies are invalid. Existing eligible cognitive/social, music-behavior and image well-being studies are not deleted merely because their outcomes differ: they are placed in the same outcome matrix.
